ORBIT — A LITTLE SPACE TO FOCUS

Requires macOS 14 or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Drag Orbit.app onto Applications, then open Orbit from Applications.

This showcase build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized. macOS may ask
you to approve it in System Settings > Privacy & Security after the first
launch attempt. Only approve the build if you trust its source.

Choose Stellar, Synthwave, or Cyberpunk using the theme menu in the lower
left corner. Each theme has gradient backgrounds, a gradient timer dial,
and a gradient start button.

Space starts or pauses. Command-R resets. Command-comma opens Settings.
Shift-Command-M switches between the full timer and floating mini mode.
The mini timer shares the same session, stays above other windows, and
remembers its position. Its session menu also lets you choose a theme.

Living themes animate while running and shift palettes during breaks.
Effects respect macOS Reduce Motion and can be disabled in Settings.
Settings includes editable session lengths, sound, keep-on-top, and a
10-second demo to show the full session-completion flow.

Every fourth completed focus session earns a long break. Start each new
session yourself. The timer tracks elapsed time across sleep and restores
its state on relaunch. A sound and in-app message mark completion.

No accounts, network calls, external dependencies, or analytics.
